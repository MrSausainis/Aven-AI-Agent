Accessibility

Current accessibility approach and known limitations for the AvenAI website.

Last updated: 29 September 2026
PrivacyTermsRefundsTrackingSecurityAccessibility

Current support

  • Keyboard-visible focus states.
  • Keyboard-operable account tabs and navigation.
  • Skip-to-content links.
  • Improved contrast for secondary labels.
  • Reduced-motion support.
  • Labels for form fields and review rating controls.
  • Responsive small-screen layouts.

Known limitations

The website has not yet undergone a formal WCAG conformance audit, and the desktop app has separate accessibility work.

Feedback

Report accessibility problems through the project issue tracker without including sensitive personal data.
