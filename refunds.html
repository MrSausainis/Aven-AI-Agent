Refunds & withdrawal

Plain-language information about subscription cancellation, refunds and EU withdrawal rights for Jysen AI

Last updated: 1 October 2026
PrivacyTermsRefundsTrackingSecurityAccessibility

1. Subscriptions

Paid Jysen AI plans, when offered, are recurring subscriptions processed by Stripe. Cancelling stops future renewal; access normally continues until the end of the paid billing period unless a refund, reversal or applicable law requires otherwise.

2. EU withdrawal rights

EU consumers can have a statutory withdrawal period for distance contracts. The exact treatment of digital content/services depends on how and when performance begins. Jysen AI will only rely on a loss of withdrawal rights where legally required express consent, acknowledgement and confirmation have been obtained.

The current beta checkout flow still needs a final EU withdrawal/cancellation implementation before general paid launch. This page does not reduce any statutory right.

3. Refund requests

Duplicate charges, billing errors, unauthorized charges and service failures will be reviewed in line with applicable law and Stripe records. Statutory refund rights take priority over project policy.

4. Support

For a refund, withdrawal, cancellation or billing question, email jysenai.support@gmail.com from the email used for your account or purchase. Include a brief description and an invoice reference if available. Never send passwords, API keys or full payment-card details. See Support for the available contact channels.
