Tracking & browser storage

What the AvenAI website currently stores in your browser and why there is no advertising-tracking banner.

Last updated: 29 September 2026
PrivacyTermsRefundsTrackingSecurityAccessibility

1. Current tracking position

The website source currently loads no advertising pixels, behavioral analytics scripts or cross-site marketing trackers. Because the current site does not intentionally use non-essential tracking technologies, it does not show a consent banner solely for appearance.

2. Authentication storage

If you sign in, Supabase Auth stores session information in the browser so the account page can keep you signed in and refresh the session. This storage is used to provide the account feature you requested.

3. External services

Following a link to GitHub or Stripe takes you to those services, which may use their own cookies or storage under their own policies.

4. Future analytics

If non-essential analytics or marketing technology is added later, the site will assess whether consent is required and update this notice and consent controls before enabling it.
