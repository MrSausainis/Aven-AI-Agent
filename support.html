Private email

For account access, billing, refund or withdrawal requests, privacy questions and accessibility help, contact jysenai.support@gmail.com.

If the email link does not open your mail app, copy the address into Gmail or another email service. For account or billing requests, write from the email used for your account or purchase and include a brief description and an invoice reference if available.

The same support channels are available on every plan. There is no guaranteed response time or separate priority support service.

Never send passwords, API keys, recovery codes or full payment-card details. Remove private content from screenshots and logs before sharing them. Email opens your chosen mail service; this page does not upload your message or attachments.

Public bug reports

For a non-sensitive, reproducible bug, use the GitHub issue tracker. Include the app version, Windows version, what you did and what happened. Public issues can be read by anyone; keep account, billing and privacy requests in email.

Security reports

Report a vulnerability through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting flow, or email the support address if that flow is unavailable. Share a brief description privately and wait for a reply before sending sensitive exploit material.

Current product name

Jysen AI is the new project brand for AVEN. The current Windows installer, app and wake word still use AVEN while the transition is in progress. Existing download filenames, account connections and repository links remain the same.

Previously published Terms and Privacy versions may use the former name. Those records are preserved. The support mailbox does not mean that general paid launch or business registration is complete.
