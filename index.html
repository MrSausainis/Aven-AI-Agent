What's actually in it

◐

An overlay that stays out of the way

A small pill at the top of your screen shows what's playing, an active timer, or that it's listening — and collapses back to a dot the moment there's nothing to say.

◔

Voice, without a wake-word gimmick

Say its name and talk. Runs in the cloud for speed, or fully local on your own GPU if you'd rather nothing leave your machine.

◑

Widgets that show up only when relevant

A CS2 match clock that appears only while the game is actually focused, and a notes checklist you can star and glance at mid-game — nothing permanent parked on your screen.

◕

Themes that actually change how it feels

Five palettes, each with its own animation character — some calm and slow, some sharp and immediate — not just a repainted accent color.
